Category-A 20-gene similarity analysis summary
============================================================

Method
------
- Query: 20 genes flagged 'category contains A' in
  tables/exposed_TF_notable_list.tsv (TSS+/-500bp methylation
  change AND |log2FC|>=1 AND same direction).
- Protein sequences: extracted from
  /Users/okaban/bioinfo/methyl/260102_M145/data/ref.gbk (NCBI RefSeq
  GCF_000203835.1, NC_003888.3) using locus_tag/old_locus_tag.
- Pfam domains: hmmscan vs Pfam-A.hmm (E<=1e-3).
- Sequence similarity: blastp vs 6 curated literature references
  (UniProt fetch). Tabular output, e-value threshold 10 (loose).
- Functional similarity rule: a query is called 'family analogue'
  of a literature reference if its Pfam accession set overlaps the
  reference family group (see FAMILY_GROUPS in script).

Headline numbers
----------------
- 20 / 20 query proteins extracted (no missing).
- 10 / 20 share Pfam family with at least one literature reference.
- 3 / 20 have a BLAST hit with E<1e-3 to any literature reference.
- 1 / 20 also reach pident>=30% on that hit.

Per-family count (Pfam-overlap basis)
-------------------------------------
-  1  MarR (E. coli MarR; methylation-context regulator)
-  1  LysR/OxyR (E. coli OxyR; agn43 Dam-switch)
-  0  Lrp/AsnC (E. coli Lrp; pap GATC switch)
-  1  Sigma-70 ECF (M. tuberculosis SigB; m6A-near-σ-box)
-  3  TCS Response Regulator GerE/Receiver (S. coelicolor RamR analogue)
-  4  TetR/AcrR (Casadesús & Low 2006 review; methylation-coupled repressors)

What this supports (defensible claims)
--------------------------------------
- The category-A 20 genes cover regulator families that are
  documented as methylation-coupled in other organisms
  (MarR, LysR/OxyR-like, Lrp/AsnC, sigma-70 ECF, TCS GerE-RR,
  TetR/AcrR). This is a *Pfam-family* analogy, not orthology.
- Specific examples (see TSV for full list):
    * SCO3134 (SC_RS17795) [Response regulator] -> TCS Response Regulator GerE/Receiver (S. coelicolor RamR analogue)
    * SCO6685 (SC_RS35610) [Response regulator] -> TCS Response Regulator GerE/Receiver (S. coelicolor RamR analogue)
    * SCO5956 (SC_RS32025) [TetR] -> TetR/AcrR (Casadesús & Low 2006 review; methylation-coupled repressors)
    * SCO2223 (SC_RS13145) [TetR] -> TetR/AcrR (Casadesús & Low 2006 review; methylation-coupled repressors)
    * SCO4122 (SC_RS22780) [MarR] -> MarR (E. coli MarR; methylation-context regulator)
    * SCO0089 (SC_RS02455) [LysR] -> LysR/OxyR (E. coli OxyR; agn43 Dam-switch)
    * SCO7314 (SC_RS38785) [Sigma factor] -> Sigma-70 ECF (M. tuberculosis SigB; m6A-near-σ-box)
    * SCO0877 (SC_RS06310) [HTH (other)] -> TCS Response Regulator GerE/Receiver (S. coelicolor RamR analogue)
    * SCO4639 (SC_RS25370) [TetR] -> TetR/AcrR (Casadesús & Low 2006 review; methylation-coupled repressors)
    * SCO6299 (SC_RS33745) [TetR] -> TetR/AcrR (Casadesús & Low 2006 review; methylation-coupled repressors)

What this does NOT support
--------------------------
- High BLAST identity / orthology to E. coli MarR, OxyR, Lrp,
  H. pylori HP1021, or M. tuberculosis SigB. All best hits are
  weak (mostly pident<35% over short stretches; most E>1e-3).
  Streptomyces TFs are too divergent for direct sequence orthology.
- A claim that the *mechanism* is the same. Only the Pfam family
  is shared. Whether Dam/Dcm-style methylation actually toggles
  these regulators in S. coelicolor must be tested directly
  (e.g. mutate the methyl site, ChIP-seq, EMSA).

Caveats on the literature reference set
---------------------------------------
- UniProt Q9XAP2 (labelled 'RamR' in some databases) is in fact
  RamC (Radical_SAM + CofH/MqnC), NOT the SCO6685 response
  regulator. Its BLAST/Pfam comparison is therefore not
  meaningful as a TCS-RR positive control. We instead use the
  GerE+Response_reg Pfam pair as the canonical TCS-RR family.
- UniProt O25617 (HP1021) returned no strong Pfam hit at E<=1e-3.
  HP1021 family classification therefore relies on the literature
  rather than a Pfam-level matching here.
